About the Event

Teachers' Day is celebrated in college to express gratitude and respect towards teachers for their guidance and support. Students organize various activities such as cultural programs, speeches, games, dances, and small gifts for their teachers. It is a joyful occasion where students and teachers come together, creating a warm and memorable atmosphere. The celebration reminds us of the important role teachers play in shaping our knowledge, skills, and future.
