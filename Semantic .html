About Department

Vision

To be a premier center of academic excellence and research in computer science, empowering students to lead and innovate globally.

Mission

To deliver high-quality technical education, foster critical thinking, and encourage interdisciplinary research to meet evolving industry needs.

Objectives

  • Impart technical problem-solving skills.
  • Encourage ethical software practices and lifelong learning.
  • Facilitate industry-academia collaborations.
